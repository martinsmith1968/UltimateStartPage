# Ultimate Start Page

## Follow-ups and unconfirmed items go in ToDo.md

When a piece of work leaves follow-ups (known limitations, deliberately deferred parts, related bugs found along the way) or anything unconfirmed (e.g. code that couldn't be built, run or tested here), add them to `ToDo.md` in the repository root without asking.

- Add them as unticked `- [ ]` sub-items under the item they relate to, or as a new item if there isn't one.
- Prefix unconfirmed items with "Unconfirmed:" and say what needs checking and how.
- Tick `- [x]` items you fix, with a short note of what was done.
- Mention in your reply what you added.
